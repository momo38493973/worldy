Japanese Woodblock Print — Nagasena Summons the Dragon Bowl by Utagawa Kuniyoshi (1836)

Seated on the rocks, Nagasena raises a bowl and a blue dragon surges out with coiled force. He remains still while the dragon twists above him with open jaws and claws.

In Japan, dragons are tied to rain, water, protection, and authority. Here, power does not come from rage. It feels drawn from prayer, discipline, and long training.

Kuniyoshi makes stillness command the wild.
