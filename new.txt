*Japanese Woodblock Print — Shishimai Nekozoku by Ishiguro Akiko*

At the center, a great guardian cat steps forward with thunder in its paws. One eye gold, one eye blue, flames bloom from its ears, and a sacred bell hangs from its neck. Around it, smaller cats dance, play trumpets, and carry charms like a living parade.

In Japan, cats are tied to luck, protection, and warding off evil spirits. The shishi-like mane and fierce gaze turn playfulness into power. Here, strength does not come from destruction. It comes from joy, mischief, and the spirit of community.

Ishiguro makes chaos feel sacred.
